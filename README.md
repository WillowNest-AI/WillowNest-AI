# What WillowNest does

- 51 evidence-based chapters — from conception to birth, written in plain language
- Willow, your AI companion — ask anything, get calm answers grounded in WHO, NHS, and VA sources
- Citation transparency — every response shows where the information came from
- Highlight and explain — select any confusing passage and get a simpler explanation
- Appointment preparation — ask what to expect at your next scan or midwife visit
- Week-personalised — Willow knows which week you're in and adjusts her answers accordingly
- Light, dark, and sepia themes — read comfortably at any time of day
- Conversation history — your chats are saved and exportable as a PDF

# How it works

1. Read a chapter at your own pace
2. Ask Willow anything about what you're reading
3. She draws on trusted health sources (WHO, NHS, VA) and shows her citations
4. Highlight a confusing paragraph and get an instant explanation
5. Prepare for appointments by asking what to expect
